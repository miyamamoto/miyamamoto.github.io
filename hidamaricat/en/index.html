Sunny Spot Cat

A little cat that lives on top of your windows (Mac and Windows).

FAQ

  • Where is the menu? On Mac, click the paw in the menu bar. On Windows, click the paw in the notification area (inside “^” if hidden) or right-click the cat.
  • How do I use it? Choose “Try the Tutorial” in the menu for a step-by-step guide.
  • I can’t see the cat. Check that “Play on Windows” is on in the menu. With no windows open, the cat sits on the Dock or taskbar.
  • Do you collect data? No. Everything stays on your device.
  • Does it cost anything? All features are free. “Support” is optional.
