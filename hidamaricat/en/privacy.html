Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Sunny Spot Cat (“the app”) is provided by Mitsuo Yamamoto.

We collect no data

The app does not collect personal information, usage data, location, or advertising identifiers, and sends nothing to the developer or any third party. There is no account, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Information used only on your device

  • CPU and memory usage, and the positions and sizes of other apps’ windows (so the cat can play on top of them).
  • The positions of other menu bar icons and the names of the apps that own them (so the cat stays clear of them).
  • The name of the app that owns the window the cat is sitting on (the bundle ID on Mac, the program file name on Windows), to pick a matching scene, such as listening to music on a music app.
  • Window contents and window titles are never read.
  • Your chosen cat and settings (stored only in the app’s preferences on your device).

None of this ever leaves your device.

In-app purchases

Purchases under “Support” are processed by Apple (App Store) or Microsoft (Microsoft Store). Neither the app nor the developer receives your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether a purchase is active.

Time used

To show the optional “Support” request only to people who have used the app for a while, the app records the total time it has been used in its preferences on your device only. It is never sent anywhere.
